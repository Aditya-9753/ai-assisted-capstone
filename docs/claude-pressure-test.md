# Claude Sitemap Pressure-Test Prompt

Use this exact prompt inside the configured **AI-Assisted Python Portfolio** Claude Project conversation to pressure-test the sitemap.

## Pressure-Test Prompt

```text
Act as my portfolio tutor and pressure-test my sitemap.

My proof statement is:

I am a Python developer who builds reliable applications, REST APIs, and backend systems, and my portfolio should prove my ability to solve real-world business problems through clean, scalable software.

My target audience is:

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential employers looking for a Python developer.

My primary action is:

Contact me for a development opportunity.

My proposed sitemap is:

Home
├── Hero
├── Work / Case Studies
├── About
└── Contact

Evaluate this sitemap against:

1. My proof statement
2. My target audience
3. The visitor journey
4. The single primary action
5. Whether every section earns its place
6. Whether anything is unnecessary
7. Whether anything important is missing
8. Whether the structure helps prove my capabilities rather than simply describing them

Be critical rather than simply agreeing with me.

Give me:

1. What works
2. What does not work
3. At least 3 specific recommendations
4. The ONE change you think I should make before building the portfolio

Then explain why that change matters.
```
